# furnace-rs Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The furnace-rs community welcomes people of every background and experience
level. Everyone participating in the project, including maintainers, is expected
to help make collaboration respectful, accessible, and free from harassment.
This includes respect regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
or expression, nationality, religion, sexual orientation, socioeconomic status,
or technical experience.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and assume good intent while addressing harmful
  behavior when it occurs.
- Give constructive feedback about code and ideas. Explain disagreements
  without attacking the person proposing them.
- Welcome questions and help newcomers learn the project conventions.
- Respect personal boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes, listen to feedback, and correct harmful
  behavior.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, sexualized comments or unwanted advances, sustained disruption,
publishing someone else's private information without permission, and
retaliation against anyone who reports a concern or participates in a review.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions,
and other spaces managed by furnace-rs. It also applies when someone represents
the project publicly. Conduct outside those spaces may be considered when it
directly threatens a participant's safety or ability to participate here.

## Reporting a concern

Contact a repository maintainer through a private contact method they publish.
If no private method is available, open an
[issue requesting a private reporting channel](https://github.com/Adriannathan89/furnace-rs/issues/new).
Keep that request free of incident details, names, and personal information;
share the report once a private channel is established.

Include what happened, where and when it occurred, relevant links or evidence,
and any immediate concerns. Share only information needed to understand the
incident. If the concern involves a maintainer, ask an uninvolved maintainer
to handle it where one is available.

Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and share information only as
needed to assess and address the incident. Absolute confidentiality cannot be
guaranteed. Reporting in good faith must not result in retaliation.

For software vulnerabilities, follow the separate
[security reporting policy](docs/SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove inappropriate content and restrict participation to
protect the community. Depending on severity, impact, and repeated behavior,
actions may include a request to correct behavior, a warning, a temporary
restriction, or a permanent ban. Serious threats or harassment may justify
immediate restrictions without a prior warning.

Maintainers should consider relevant context, explain decisions to affected
participants when appropriate, and avoid disclosing private report details.
Anyone with a conflict of interest should step back from the decision.

An affected participant may request reconsideration through the same private
reporting channel, providing relevant context or new information. An uninvolved
maintainer should review the request where one is available.
